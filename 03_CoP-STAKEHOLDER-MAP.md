# **HCSC Cloud Product Intake & Governance**

## **Stakeholder Roles by Community of Practice**

---

## **Standard Roles Inside Each Community of Practice (CoP)**

| **Role**                                      | **Description**                                                                                                                               |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CoP Lead** (sometimes called Owner)         | Accountable for the CoP's deliverables, governance, backlog prioritization, and representing the CoP at cross-functional leadership meetings. |
| **Technical Lead / SME**                      | Subject matter expert; provides hands-on architectural and technical leadership for key capabilities owned by the CoP.                        |
| **Process Steward**                           | Maintains intake workflows, ticket handling procedures, and escalation paths for work handled by the CoP.                                     |
| **Compliance/Standards Liaison**              | Ensures the CoP's outputs align with security, risk, regulatory, and enterprise standards. Works closely with Governance.                     |
| **Operations/Delivery Manager**               | Responsible for day-to-day execution, team-level sprint tracking, tool management, and reporting progress on deliverables.                    |
| **Contributor / Practitioner**                | Performs the work: engineering, validation, documentation, reviews, troubleshooting, etc. Can be generalist or specialist roles.              |
| **Product Manager / Service Owner Interface** | Ensures that CoP outputs connect to customer-facing services and products properly, aligned with broader cloud catalog goals.                 |
| **Observer/Advisor (Optional)**               | Provides guidance but not operational execution; may come from Risk, Audit, Legal, or external consultative teams.                            |

# **Key Notes:**

* **Scalability:** This structure allows each CoP to be small and tactical or large and federated depending on the business need.
* **Governance Fit:** Compliance liaisons create a strong mesh with regulatory frameworks (HIPAA, HITRUST, etc.).
* **Agile Alignment:** Delivery managers and process stewards enable agile Kanban/sprint operations within CoPs.
* **Cross-CoP Collaboration:** Product Managers/Interfaces ensure outputs of one CoP integrate into broader services and governance models.

---

# **Summary**

> **Each Community of Practice at HCSC has a clear set of standardized high-level roles, specialized to match their specific operational focus.**
> This ensures every intake, governance, and cloud product service has accountable owners, measurable workflows, and full compliance across the entire hybrid multi-cloud ecosystem.
