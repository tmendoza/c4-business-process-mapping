# **Center of Excellence (CoE) Role Structure — Abstracted for Business Service Containers**

---

## **Standard Roles Inside Each Center of Excellence (CoE / Business Service Area)**

| **Role**                                 | **Description**                                                                                                                              |
| :--------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Service Area Owner (CoE Leader)**      | Accountable for the entire service area’s outcomes: intake, governance, delivery, continuous improvement. Strategic leadership.              |
| **Service Delivery Manager**             | Oversees execution across multiple Communities of Practice; ensures services meet SLAs and governance standards.                             |
| **Governance Coordinator**               | Ensures compliance to organizational policies, security frameworks, financial controls, and regulatory requirements inside the service area. |
| **Service Architects (Lead Architects)** | Design and validate the architectures, integrations, and service delivery models for the services managed by the CoE.                        |
| **Technical Program Manager**            | Runs coordination of cross-functional initiatives inside the CoE — process improvement, onboarding new products, integrations.               |
| **Service Portfolio Analyst**            | Tracks all active services/products inside the CoE, lifecycle statuses, ownership assignments, and metrics reporting.                        |
| **Cross-CoP Liaison**                    | Interface between CoPs inside the CoE and external teams (e.g., Security, Governance Board, other CoEs).                                     |
| **Support/Operational Leader**           | Owns day-to-day monitoring, operational health, incident management, and support escalation for services in the CoE.                         |
| **Executive Sponsor (Optional)**         | Senior leadership advocate for the CoE's initiatives, priorities, and funding across the enterprise.                                         |

# **Why This Layered Abstraction Works**

* **Adaptable:** Can apply to any healthcare, finance, insurance, or regulated cloud shop.
* **Clear Ownership:** Every Center of Excellence has unambiguous accountability for service, governance, and operational health.
* **Support for Agile/DevOps:** Allows for lightweight coordination of cross-functional service streams inside each CoE.
* **Enterprise-Ready:** Easy to map these into org charts, RACI matrices, or architectural governance boards.

# **Summary**

> Each **Center of Excellence** in HCSC’s Cloud Product Intake and Governance Model operates under a standard, scalable role structure — aligned with the C4 model of organizational architecture — ensuring enterprise-wide clarity, accountability, and compliance.
