# **Operational Playbooks and Checklists Needed for Cloud Product Intake and Governance**

---

## **Intake & Discovery Phase Playbooks**

1. **TAP Request Submission Playbook**

   * How Application Teams submit new Technology Approval Requests properly.

2. **Intake Triage and Validation Playbook**

   * How Cloud Product Management CoP triages and validates incoming TAPs.
   * Decision logic for Discovery needed vs. move forward.

3. **Discovery Execution Playbook**

   * How to conduct a formal Discovery process.
   * Roles, activities (PoC/PoV), Discovery Document creation.

4. **Discovery Completion Checklist**

   * Checklist to validate that Discovery outcomes are complete, documented, and ready to proceed to Concept Note.

---

## **Architecture, Security, Cost Evaluation Phase Playbooks**

5. **Architecture Review Playbook**

   * How Enterprise Architecture CoP performs technical reviews for cloud products.

6. **Security Risk Assessment Playbook**

   * How Security CoP assesses compliance, risk, threat models.

7. **Cloud Cost Validation Playbook (FinOps)**

   * How FinOps CoP models cost estimates and performs budget validation.

8. **Request for Comment (RFC) Development Checklist**

   * Checklist for assembling complete RFC documentation across architecture, security, and cost findings.

---

## **Governance Gate Phase Playbooks**

9. **Governance Gate Review Playbook**

   * How Governance Board reviews Concept Note + RFC, leads decision making, records approvals or rejections.

10. **Governance Gate Approval Checklist**

    * Checklist of mandatory artifacts and validations required for Blueprint sign-off.

---

## **Provisioning, Observability, and Operationalization Phase Playbooks**

11. **Infrastructure Provisioning Playbook**

    * How Platform Engineering CoP provisions cloud products based on approved Blueprints.

12. **Observability and Monitoring Setup Playbook**

    * How telemetry and monitoring integrations (Cribl, Dynatrace) are configured at deployment.

13. **Operational Handoff Checklist**

    * Checklist to ensure product is operationally ready to be handed off to Service Ownership (runbooks, telemetry, alerts configured).

---

## **Ongoing Service Management Playbooks**

14. **Service Ownership and Lifecycle Management Playbook**

    * How Service Ownership CoP manages the active service lifecycle (SLAs, incidents, upgrades, retirements).

15. **Incident Response and RCA Playbook**

    * How incidents, root cause analysis (RCA), and compliance escalations are handled post-launch.

16. **Periodic Compliance Audit Checklist**

    * Checklist to verify service is still compliant with regulatory and governance standards (HIPAA, HITRUST, internal controls).
