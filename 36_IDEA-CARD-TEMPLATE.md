# **Confluence Template 7: Idea Card**

**Template Title:**

> Idea Card

**Purpose:**

> Capture a **simple, business-focused summary** of an approved cloud service or product, to inform **Corporate Finance**, **Portfolio Management**, and **Executive Leadership** about funding prioritization — based on strategic value, urgency, and cost impact.

This format is **intentionally light and executive-friendly** — very different from the deep technical RFC or Blueprint.
