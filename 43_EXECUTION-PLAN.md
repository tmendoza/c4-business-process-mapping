# **High-Level Rollout Timeline (12 Weeks Total)**

*(Focused on delivering incrementally every \~2 weeks.)*

---

## **Weeks 1–2: Foundation Build**

* Create Confluence Space.
* Build Landing Page (skeleton).
* Load all Templates (TAP, Discovery, Concept Note, RFC, etc.).
* Set up initial Intake and RFC Trackers (empty but working).
* Wire action buttons to templates.

**Milestone:**

> Foundation Space Live — Ready to intake internal rollout TAPs.

---

## **Weeks 2–4: Intake + Discovery Workflows**

* Submit TAPs for each major buildout piece (e.g., Landing Page v1, TAP Tracker MVP).
* Start Discovery Documents where more research is needed.
* Develop initial Concept Notes for dashboards, compliance trackers.

**Milestone:**

> TAP Tracker and Discovery Process active.
> Concept Notes moving toward RFCs.

---

## **Weeks 4–6: Governance and Approvals**

* Submit first real RFCs (e.g., "Landing Page Launch RFC", "TAP Tracker RFC").
* Conduct first internal Governance Reviews.
* Approve first Cloud Governance Blueprints.

**Milestone:**

> First artifacts fully governed and approved.
> "Eat our own dog food" milestone.

---

## **Weeks 6–8: Full Operational Buildout**

* Expand TAP, RFC, and Blueprint dashboards.
* Build RCA Repository Tracker.
* Build Compliance Evidence Tracker.
* Finalize Runbook Templates for operational handoffs.

**Milestone:**

> Core operational dashboards live.
> Service lifecycle management artifacts created.

---

## **Weeks 8–10: Training and Internal Soft Launch**

* Conduct 2–3 training sessions (submit TAPs, track RFCs, use Governance Portal).
* Create Knowledge Base Quick Guides.
* Issue Launch Communications internally (PMs, Engineers, Architects, Security).

**Milestone:**

> Cloud Governance Space live for internal projects.
> Teams trained and beginning usage.

---

## **Weeks 10–12: Stabilization and Feedback Loop**

* Monitor first 3–5 TAPs through full intake-governance-build cycles.
* Conduct feedback sessions ("What's working? What's confusing?").
* Launch Round 2 Enhancements (adjust templates, dashboards as needed).

**Milestone:**

> Governance Space stabilized.
> Continuous improvement cycle initiated.

# **First Epic:**

> **Epic Name:**
> *"Rollout Cloud Product Governance Platform - Phase 1"*

> Build the foundational components of the HCSC Cloud Product Governance and Operations Platform, enabling intake of service requests, governance workflows, and operational lifecycle tracking using Confluence and Agile methods.

# **User Stories under Epic**

---

| **User Story**                         | **Description**                                                                          | **Acceptance Criteria**                                                 |
| :------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
